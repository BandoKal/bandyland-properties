## Source and scheduling rules

The plan contains a 30-day conversion sequence, but actual prerequisites govern launch and possession. The proposed November 6 listing yields November 13 and November 20 demand reviews. A fallback review occurs at least seven days after an actual price change. Neither a signed lease nor occupancy is assumed by day 30.

BandyWorks and property-equipment dates are proposed sequencing of the workbook's purchase dependencies. No document supplies firm vendor delivery dates. Keep work blocked until required approvals, quotes, installation checks and funding exist.

P0 means a funding, legal, insurance, safety or execution prerequisite; P1 means planned execution work; P2 means a purchase that can follow the critical prerequisites. Priority alone does not make a blocked task executable.
