## What each platform provides

### 1. Avail — first demonstration

Applications and screening organize applicant information; listing distribution supports marketing; leases and e-signatures keep signed terms together; maintenance requests create a repair history; income/expense tracking supports monthly reconciliation. Plus adds customizations and eligible FastPay. Coordinate listings with Amanda to avoid duplicate or inconsistent advertisements. [Feature comparison](https://www.avail.com/pricing).

Avail explicitly supports routing rent/fees and security deposits to different bank accounts. This matters because tenant deposits must stay separate from owner reserves. Tenants can upload insurance documentation. Partner lease signing is documented, but that does **not establish independent day-to-day management permissions** for Kara. [Bank routing](https://support.avail.com/article/manage-deposits-to-different-bank-accounts), [insurance uploads](https://support.avail.com/article/upload-renters-insurance-documentation), [partner signing](https://support.avail.com/article/sign-lease-as-partners).

**Before choosing:** confirm separate logins with usable backup permissions, CreditBoost terms, settlement timing, and tenant-facing ACH fees. A shared password is not the proposed backup procedure.

### 2. RentRedi — strongest operational alternative

Includes unlimited teammates, tenant communication, applications, leases, maintenance, and accounting tools in its advertised plan. This is useful for Jason delegating to Kara without losing the shared repair/payment record. [Plan features](https://rentredi.com/pricing).

Owner can absorb the $1 ACH fee and disable credit/debit card payments. Cards otherwise cost 3.1% + $0.30. Payment processing is generally 2–3 business days, with verification potentially taking longer. Confirm permission limits and separate deposit routing during the demonstration. [Payment settings](https://help.rentredi.com/en/articles/8473991-rentredi-payments-faqs).

### 3. TurboTenant — explicit two-person roles

Pro combines fee-free ACH with paid leasing/accounting features and multiple linked banks. Its team roles let another person act as Admin or Assistant; outbound messages display the primary account's name, so agree how Jason/Kara identify themselves in messages. [Pricing](https://www.turbotenant.com/pricing/), [team roles](https://support.turbotenant.com/en/articles/9690662-team-roles-how-to-add-additional-users-to-your-turbotenant-account).

The live pricing page showed $16.58/month starting annual pricing; some indexed material differed. Use the checkout total before committing. Card payments carry a 3.49% tenant fee; free ACH does not make cards free. [Payment processing](https://support.turbotenant.com/en/articles/4522996-how-long-do-rent-payments-take-to-process).

### 4. Innago — low-cost candidate

No subscription fee for the core rent, lease, screening, maintenance, and financial-reporting tools. ACH costs $2 and cards 2.99%; the landlord can absorb processing fees. Records can be exported. Demonstrate Kara's permissions, Wells Fargo connection, separate deposit routing, and insurance-document handling before selecting. [Official FAQ](https://innago.com/faq/).

Current marketing advertises three-bureau reporting and historical payments, while an older official article describes TransUnion-only reporting, no history, and free service. Treat current enrollment cost, negative reporting, and history eligibility as **unresolved**, not as a promised free benefit. [Current reporting product](https://innago.com/tenant-credit-reporting/), [older reporting article](https://innago.com/build-better-credit-with-innago-free-tenant-credit-reporting/).

### 5. Baselane — bookkeeping-focused candidate

Smart adds shared access and more accounting automation; external bank accounts are supported, so choosing Baselane does not require moving your Wells Fargo accounts. It can collect rent and support financial records, but a complete maintenance-ticket workflow was not verified; use a separate repair log unless demonstrated. [Pricing](https://www.baselane.com/pricing), [shared access](https://support.baselane.com/hc/en-us/articles/29873891217307-How-to-Share-Access-and-Invite-Additional-Users-to-Your-Baselane-Workspace).

External-bank ACH costs $2, payable by owner or tenant. Waiving it through Baselane checking is an alternative, not a reason by itself to open more accounts. Cards cost tenants 3.49%. Published settlement timing is generally five business days, or two for eligible Smart payments. [Collection options](https://www.baselane.com/rent-collection).

## Rent reporting: a separate, optional tenant benefit

Reporting rent may help establish credit history; it does not guarantee a score increase or that every lender's scoring model uses it. Tenant consent/enrollment and correct identities matter. Do not enroll tenants automatically or describe an optional reporting fee as a payment fee.

If you fund reporting, confirm whether the provider supports landlord payment or whether a documented reimbursement is needed. Costs are per enrolled person, not necessarily per household. Subscription/ACH spending and any tenant-benefit allowance are separate budget lines.

## What are cards for?

Cards are an optional way for a tenant to pay rent with a credit or debit card instead of a bank transfer. They are not required to connect your bank or use online payments. A credit card can also expose the tenant to interest from the card issuer. For your stated preference, offer **ACH with $0 tenant processing fee** and clearly explain any optional card charge. RentRedi explicitly permits disabling cards; verify controls on other platforms. Never advertise every payment method as free when only ACH is free.
