# Rental plan: shared overview

Public reading edition • Updated October 26, 2026

This summary reflects current owner decisions. The original business plan (the six-page reference) and budget remain private workspace files because they contain financial details. This is not a reproduction of those files or a substitute for them.

## 1. What we are working toward

Prepare the house for a residential tenancy, select a practical rental platform, and establish a manageable routine for Jason and Kara. The property will be self-managed. Actual listing, lease and move-in dates are not yet recorded here; proposed dates are planning targets.

## 2. Who does what

Jason is the accountable manager. Kara is the backup and can take delegated work. Amanda will handle listing on real-estate platforms and remains the agent if the owners choose to sell. Responsibility decisions are established; contacts, access and operating procedures still need configuration.

## 3. Property readiness

Review existing records and the recent appraisal, then identify remaining safety, mechanical and condition checks. An appraisal is not a completed safety inspection. Log necessary repairs, retain invoices and take condition photos. Jason plans to close the pool using supplies already owned. The pool remains unavailable to tenants; written insurer requirements are outstanding.

## 4. Tenant experience

Choose a platform that supports online rent, a fee-free ACH option for tenants, maintenance requests and organized documents. Review screening criteria, lease terms and notices consistently. Evaluate optional rent reporting with tenant consent and clear costs. Insurance proof requirements and any proposed tenant benefits still need review before they become lease promises.

## 5. Day-to-day operation

Agree on routine and emergency contact channels, backup coverage, repair approval and vendor contacts. Keep tenant deposits separate from operating funds and reserves. Reconcile monthly records and maintain reminders for inspections, insurance evidence and renewals. Funding completion is owner-confirmed; detailed balances and financial records are not published here.

## 6. How we move forward

Keep no more than three items In progress. Work waiting on someone else moves On hold with a reason and follow-up. Complete the current records, lender and platform work before taking on more active tasks. Marketing and tenancy reviews run from actual events, not an assumed calendar date. BandyWorks and unrelated equipment purchases are outside this project.
